After more than six decades of conflict, 137,000 people have been reported missing in Colombia. A partnership between The Ohio State University’s new Mershon Search Lab and Colombia’s Unidad de Búsqueda de Personas dadas por Desaparecidas (UBPD) aims to identify the missing and bring closure to their families.
