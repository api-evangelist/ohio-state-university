The Ohio State University Marching Band welcomed fans back to Ohio Stadium Saturday with a celebration of America’s 250th birthday. Musical selections from the performance during the Ball State game’s halftime included “Yankee Doodle,” “America the Beautiful,” “America (My Country ‘Tis of Thee),” “Stars and Stripes Forever,” and more.
