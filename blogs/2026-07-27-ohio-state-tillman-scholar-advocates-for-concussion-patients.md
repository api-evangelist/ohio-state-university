Thomas “T.J.” Beach, an 8-year veteran of the U.S. Marine Corps, is The Ohio State University’s 12th Tillman Scholar. Now, the central Ohio native is merging his experience in the armed forces with his education as an athletic trainer to help law enforcement and others recover from concussions.
