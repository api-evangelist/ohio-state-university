Over the summer, representatives from more than 1,700 cities around the world gathered in San Sebastián, Spain, for the World Health Organization’s (WHO) 3rd World Congress of Age-Friendly Cities and Communities. Among those participants were three people from the Age-Friendly Innovation Center (AFIC) at The Ohio State University.
