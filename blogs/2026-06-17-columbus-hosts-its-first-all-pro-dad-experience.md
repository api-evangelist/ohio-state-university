Nearly 1,300 fathers, mothers, grandparents, coaches and kids stormed the Woody Hayes Athletic Center (WHAC) at The Ohio State University this week to participate in the first Columbus-area All Pro Dad Experience . Entering through an inflatable tunnel, guests had the chance to meet Brutus, run obstacle courses, create their own touchdown dances and bond as families.
